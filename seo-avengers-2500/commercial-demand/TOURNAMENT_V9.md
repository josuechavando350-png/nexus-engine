# NEXUS Commercial Demand Tournament V9

## Purpose

V9 converts the certified planning stack into an **execution sequence** without crossing into production mutation.

The question is no longer “how many pages can we invent?” V5 already established a 40-page offer-fit frontier, V6 tested paid-search capacity, V7 exposed the missing first-party funnel evidence, and V8 defined the measurement contract required before conversion claims can become empirical.

V9 therefore asks a narrower operational question:

> If the 40-page commercial architecture is eventually implemented, in what deterministic order should it be released so the highest modeled incremental demand capacity is addressed first without double-counting semantic families?

The answer is a five-wave plan of eight pages each. This is implementation priority only. It is **not** a rank forecast, traffic forecast, client forecast, revenue forecast, or probability forecast.

## Certified baseline

V9 fails closed unless the upstream stack still provides:

- V5 selected strategy: `OFFER_FIT_FULL_VALIDATED_40`.
- 40 explicit commercial/decision pages.
- 776.70 modeled relevant organic sessions/month under the existing bounded planning assumptions.
- V8 verdict: `MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT_READY_OBSERVED_COHORTS_STILL_REQUIRED`.
- V8 production instrumentation still not installed by the planning tournament.

V9 conserves the V5 modeled capacity exactly. It does not add new keyword volume, new semantic families, new CTR assumptions, or new conversion assumptions.

## Deterministic family assignment

Each non-informational demand family is assigned exactly once to a selected page.

When a family appears on more than one page, V9 assigns it to the selected page with the fewest declared demand families, then uses stable page ID as the tie-break. This prevents commercial hubs and comparison pages from being credited again for demand already assigned to a dedicated page.

Pages are then ranked by:

1. assigned modeled relevant sessions, descending;
2. assigned strict-commercial modeled sessions, descending;
3. assigned representative raw search volume, descending;
4. stable page ID.

Informational families cannot enter execution capacity.

Pages that contribute zero incremental modeled demand after deduplication are preserved when they still belong to the certified architecture, but are labeled `ARCHITECTURE_OR_DECISION_SUPPORT`. Zero incremental capacity is never rewritten as new demand.

## Wave architecture

V9 produces five waves of eight pages.

The deterministic first wave is:

1. `web-design`
2. `seo-agency`
3. `business-process-automation`
4. `software-development-companies`
5. `seo-consulting`
6. `software-company`
7. `whatsapp-chatbot`
8. `web-design-agency`

Under the inherited planning assumptions, those eight pages account for **546.30 modeled relevant sessions** out of the 776.70-session certified V5 capacity, or about **70.336% of the modeled capacity**.

That 546.30 figure is scenario accounting, not observed traffic. It must never be presented as “NEXUS will receive 546 visits.”

The remaining 32 pages are sequenced into four additional eight-page waves by the same deterministic rules. The complete sequence is emitted by the V9 engine and CLI rather than manually duplicated in this document.

## Measurement gate before outcome claims

V9 inherits the V8 measurement contract. Before future results can support a client-floor claim, observed evidence must join:

- `SESSION`
- `CONTACT`
- `QUALIFIED_LEAD`
- `CLOSED_CLIENT`

with the V8 deduplication, attribution, privacy, cohort, and maturity rules intact.

V9 does **not** install that instrumentation. It only makes its implementation a hard precondition for outcome claims.

## Paid-search boundary

Paid search remains blocked by V9.

The only eligible planning portfolio is the certified V6 exact-match portfolio, and any activation still requires:

- implementation of the measurement contract; and
- separate authorization.

Phrase and broad match remain ineligible for floor support until query-level intent, incrementality, and observed conversion evidence exist.

## Release gates

Every future wave is subject to the same planning gates:

- measurement contract implemented and validated;
- no duplicate or orphan funnel events;
- raw attribution fields preserved;
- content and indexability validation green;
- no client or revenue guarantee.

Passing V9 itself does not authorize a wave to be published.

## Result

V9 verdict:

`EXECUTION_SEQUENCE_READY_MEASUREMENT_AND_OBSERVED_COHORTS_STILL_REQUIRED`

What this means:

- the commercial architecture is now not only selected, but deterministically prioritized;
- modeled demand is conserved rather than re-counted across hubs and support pages;
- the highest-capacity pages can be implemented first when a separate execution decision is made;
- first-party measurement is still required before conversion-rate evidence or a 15-client floor claim can become empirical;
- paid search and production publishing remain separately gated.
