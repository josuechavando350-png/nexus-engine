The exact public, immutable Morpho Blue `Morpho.sol` source at `morpho-org/morpho-blue@8e26ca6a8dbc5089edcd67fb576248810fd2870a` (Git blob SHA1 `ab064b646bf161b1510e2575a99412cdc2a43ebb`) transfers seized collateral to `msg.sender`, executes its `onMorphoLiquidate(uint256,bytes)` callback, and **only after that** pulls repayment in the loan token using `safeTransferFrom`.
