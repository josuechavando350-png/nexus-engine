A fresh quote is not an original historical fill or a currently capturable opportunity. It is a preliminary liquidity feasibility test.

The quote is not proof of sending wallet balance, simulation or route execution. Official spec: https://docs.li.fi/li.fi-api/li.fi-api/requesting-a-quote; LI.FI official unauthenticated limit is 75 quotes/2 hours: https://help.li.fi/hc/en-us/articles/12111455848859-What-is-the-LI-FI-API-rate-limit.

HTTP402/403/429 globally disables subsequent quotes from that provider, no attempts to evade its restriction. Every response is hashed; only route/amount/gas summary is persisted, never calldata or sensitive data.
- Both providers must reproduce Base 8453 (where available), exact input/output addresses, input raw quantity, output 6-decimal units and positive amount. LI.FI must provide both estimated output and minimum after selected slippage, and explicitly remains `skipSimulation=true`. Malformed/nonpositive/contradictory outputs are NOT a route.

An output exceeding debt is not proof of real NQC profit; an output falling below debt is not a historical no-profit proof either. Quote sources are likely to vary in block, time, routing algorithms and gas accounting. There is no source-certified historical DEX price for any selected original tx.
